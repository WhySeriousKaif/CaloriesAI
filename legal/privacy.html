Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2026 Version: 1.0.0 (Codebase Audited)

1. Information We Collect

CalorieAI collects data necessary to provide personalized calorie and macro tracking:

  • Account Data: Email address and Clerk authentication IDs via Clerk authentication flow.
  • Health & Onboarding Metrics: Gender, date of birth, height (cm), weight (kg), target weight (kg), activity level, pace per week, and diet preference.
  • User Media & Meal Logs: Meal photo uploads (processed via ImageKit) and calculated meal logs (name, calories, protein, carbs, fat, logged timestamps).

2. How We Use Information

Your data is used strictly to operate and personalize CalorieAI:

  • Calculating daily metabolic targets using Mifflin-St Jeor formulas.
  • Processing meal photos with OpenAI (gpt-4o-mini) to estimate food calories and macros.
  • Rendering weekly analytics, progress trends, and daily meal history.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We transmit data to trusted technical service providers necessary to run the Service:

  • Clerk: User identity management & authentication tokens.
  • OpenAI: Image and text payloads for AI nutrition recognition.
  • ImageKit: Secure cloud storage for meal images.
  • Neon DB: Cloud PostgreSQL database for storing user profiles and meal records.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data. Tapping Delete Account in your profile executes an immediate server purge (DELETE /api/profile) that permanently erases your stored meal images from ImageKit, deletes your records in Neon DB, and deletes your Clerk account.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at [privacy@calorieai.com].
