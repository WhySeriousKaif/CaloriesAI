1. Who we are

Calora ("we", "our") operates the Calora mobile and web applications. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us.

2. Overview & core principles

We build privacy into Calora by default:

  • We collect only the data needed to estimate calories and manage your daily goals.
  • We do not sell your personal data or meal history to advertisers.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data with a single tap.

3. Information we collect

When using Calora, we collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication IDs provided through Apple or Google sign-in.
  • Body Metrics & Goals: Height, weight, target weight, activity level, goal pace, and diet preferences (classic, keto, vegan, vegetarian).
  • Meal Data: Meal names, calories, protein, carbs, fat, timestamps, and food photos.

4. Meal photos & vision processing

When you capture or upload a meal photo, the image is processed by our secure AI vision model to determine food composition and portion estimates. Photos are processed securely over encrypted TLS connections.

5. How we use your information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Calculate your personalized daily calorie and macronutrient targets.
  • Analyze food photographs and log completed meals to your timeline.
  • Provide streak analytics and progress reports in the Analytics view.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse of our AI infrastructure.

6. Data sharing & subprocessors

We work with trusted infrastructure partners to host and run Calora:

  • Clerk: Secure user authentication and account management.
  • ImageKit: Fast, secure image hosting and optimization.
  • OpenAI / Anthropic: Automated vision AI for meal analysis.

7. Data security & retention

Your data is encrypted both in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. We retain your logged meals and profile information for as long as your account remains active.

8. Your rights & account deletion

You have full control over your data. You may inspect, modify, or permanently delete your account at any time from the Profile tab in the app. Account deletion instantly erases your profile, logged meals, and stored images.

9. Contact privacy team

For any privacy questions or requests, please contact our data privacy officer at privacy@calora.app.
